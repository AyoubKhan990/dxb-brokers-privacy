Privacy Policy – DXB Brokers

Last updated: 12 November 2026. Contact: ak27223895@gmail.com.

What the app does

DXB Brokers helps a real-estate lead provider contact licensed Dubai real-estate brokers and brokerage offices. It lists business contact details and opens WhatsApp or your email app with a message already written. You always press Send yourself; the app never sends messages on its own.

Data in the app

  • Broker and office details (name, licence number, office, business phone, business email) come from public registers and public business directories. They are included in the app and stored only on your device.
  • Your own data: the name you enter to sign messages, statuses, notes, daily limits, your optional PIN (stored as a salted hash) and a log of which contacts you opened. All of it stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server. It does not upload any data. Device backup of the app's data is turned off.

Sharing

Data leaves your device only when you choose to open WhatsApp or your email app; the message you send is then handled under those apps' own privacy policies.

Opt-out

Every message includes an opt-out line. People who ask not to be contacted are marked "Do not contact" and the app disables messaging them. A broker who wants their details removed can write to ak27223895@gmail.com.

Deleting data

Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your device.
